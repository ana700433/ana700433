# Startup Due Diligence Scorecard

A simple scoring approach for early-stage startups, based on my work as a Genisys Venture Analyst at Illinois Tech's Kaplan Institute (Feb to May 2024).

## Context

- Evaluated 50+ startups using a data-driven framework
- 75% recommendation success rate
- Worked with founders on market sizing and growth strategy
- Also represented IIT at the Venture Capital Investment Competition (VCIC) 2024

## How the scorecard works

Each startup is rated 1 to 5 on a set of criteria. Each rating is multiplied by its weight, and the weighted total gives a score out of 5.

`scorecard_template.csv` has the criteria, example weights and space to score. Adjust the weights to fit the fund or program.

## Tools

Excel · Market research · Financial and competitive analysis

Startup names and founder materials are confidential and are not included.
